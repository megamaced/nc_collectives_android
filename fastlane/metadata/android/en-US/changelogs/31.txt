Fixes several ways your work could be lost. Text typed in the editor now survives rotating the phone or leaving the app, the formatting buttons no longer crash on the first line, and photos taken from the editor are no longer dropped. An offline edit can no longer silently overwrite a change someone else made on the server; it is kept as a draft instead. A failed upload no longer blocks later ones, and sharing no longer creates duplicate pages. Also includes security hardening.
