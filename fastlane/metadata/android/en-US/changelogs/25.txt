Attachments that aren't images now work. Tapping a PDF, office document or other file opens it in your app for that type, instead of reporting that a linked page couldn't be found, and such files render as a tappable file link rather than a blank image slot. Attachments open on tap from the grid too, and you can now attach any file type — not just photos and videos. Also fixes inline images whose reference named the attachment folder. Downloaded attachments are cleared on sign out.
